DMCA Copyright Infringement Notification Policy

Fireboy and Watergirl 6 complies with 17 U.S.C. § 512 and the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). It is our policy to respond with immediate urgency to any infringement notices and take appropriate actions under the DMCA and other applicable intellectual property laws.

If your copyrighted material has been indexed, linked to, or embedded on this portal without proper authorization and you seek its immediate removal, you must provide our Designated Copyright Agent with a written notice containing the following mandatory details:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  2. Clear identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, including registration numbers if applicable.
  3. Specific identification of the material claimed to be infringing, with the precise webpage URL(s) on our portal where the content is located.
  4. Information reasonably sufficient to permit us to contact you, such as your physical address, telephone number, and valid email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

Expedited Processing Guarantee: Send all formal DMCA notices directly to dmca.fireboywatergirl@gmail.com with the subject line "DMCA Takedown Request". Upon receipt of a legally compliant notice, our engineering staff will deactivate the embedded iframe or remove the contested URL within 24–48 hours.
