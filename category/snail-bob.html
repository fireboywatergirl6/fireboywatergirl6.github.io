Play Snail Bob Games Online for Free

Snail Bob is an award-winning physics puzzle game series featuring the adorable, slow-moving snail named Bob. In every game, Bob finds himself in a new pickle—from construction sites destroying his original home, ancient Egyptian pyramids, zero-gravity space stations, to magical fairy-tale realms!

How to Play Snail Bob

  • Continuous Movement: Bob automatically crawls forward. Click on him (or press the Spacebar) to make him stop and hide inside his shell.
  • Speed Control: Click the 2x speed button (or press the '2' key) when the path is safe to speed up Bob's crawling.
  • Turn Around: Use the reverse turn button (or press '1') to turn Bob 180 degrees.
  • Interact with the World: Click levers, buttons, bridges, laser switches, and fans to clear hazards and guide Bob safely to the exit door!
  • Collect Hidden Stars: Each level hides 3 secret stars. Find all of them to achieve a perfect 3-star rating and unlock bonus mini-games.
