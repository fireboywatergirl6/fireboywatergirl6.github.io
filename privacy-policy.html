Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This Privacy Policy documents how Fireboy and Watergirl 6 collects, utilizes, and protects information when you browse our website, play our free HTML5 games, or interact with our services.

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

At Fireboy and Watergirl 6 (accessible from https://fireboywatergirl6.github.io/), one of our primary priorities is the privacy of our visitors. We believe in transparency and data minimization: we do not mandate user registration, we do not require account passwords, and we never sell personal user information to third parties.

2. Information We Collect Automatically (Log Files)

Like almost all modern websites, Fireboy and Watergirl 6 makes use of standard server log files. The information inside the log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized where required by law)
  • Browser software and operating system type
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and timestamp of visits
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Aggregated click counts to analyze trends and prevent DDoS attacks

This information is not linked to any personally identifiable information. Its purpose is solely for administering the website, debugging server performance, and optimizing user experience.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is a primary third-party vendor on our website. Google uses cookies, specifically known as DART cookies, to serve advertisements to our site visitors based on their visit to https://fireboywatergirl6.github.io/ and other sites across the World Wide Web.

Your Advertising Choices: Visitors may choose to opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy or by modifying ad settings at Google Ads Settings.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on our site. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see.

Note that Fireboy and Watergirl 6 has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

4. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Protecting the privacy of young children on the internet is of the utmost importance to us. Fireboy and Watergirl 6 does not knowingly collect or solicit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from children under the age of 13.

If a parent, guardian, or educator believes that our website has inadvertently collected personally identifiable information of a child under the age of 13, please contact us immediately, and our team will use our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

5. European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

For visitors residing within the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific data protection rights under the GDPR, including:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected about consumers.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data collected about the consumer.
  • Demand that a business that sells a consumer's personal data, not sell the consumer's personal data (we state unequivocally: We do not sell personal data).

7. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective official websites (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Apple Safari).

8. Contacting Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact our Data Protection Officer via email at contact.fireboywatergirl@gmail.com or via our dedicated Contact Form.
