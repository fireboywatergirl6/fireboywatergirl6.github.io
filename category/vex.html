Play Vex Games Online Unblocked

Vex is the legendary stickman parkour and precision platformer series that tests your speed, agility, and reflexes. Sprint, wall-jump, slide under crushing blocks, swim through deadly water chambers, and dodge rotating sawblades across challenging Acts and Hardcore stages!

Key Parkour Moves & Mechanics

  • Wall Jumping: Jump towards walls and rapidly kick off them to scale high towers and deep chasms.
  • Slide Maneuver: Run and press Down to slide underneath low-hanging spikes and laser beams.
  • Kite & Glider: In newer Vex games, ride updrafts with hang-gliders to soar across vast chasms.
  • Checkpoints: Reach green checkpoint flags to save your progress in case of sudden traps!

Controls

Use Arrow Keys or W, A, S, D to move, jump, and crouch/slide.
Press R to restart at the last checkpoint.
