Jump Into The Plane

Jump Into The Plane is an outrageous, stunt-driven arcade game where your sole objective is to launch a supercharged stunt car off a colossal ramp and land right into the cargo bay of a flying airplane!

Game Description

Combine slingshot velocity, rocket boosters, and chaotic physics! Pull back the giant slingshot, release your car down a towering ramp, and time your booster thrusters to navigate air currents, avoid mid-air blimps, and touch down safely on the lowered cargo ramp of an airborne jet. Earn prize money to upgrade slingshot tension, rocket fuel, and flight aerodynamic wings.

How to Play & Controls

  • Launch: Click and drag back on the slingshot with the Mouse, then release to launch.
  • Rocket Booster: Press and hold Spacebar or click the Boost button while airborne to propel forward.
  • Air Steering: Use A / D Keys or Left / Right Arrows to adjust pitch and glide angle.

Tips for Success

Don't burn all your rocket boost right off the ramp lip! Wait until your car reaches the apex of its trajectory, then pulse the booster to carry your momentum straight into the cargo hold.
